Official NSS & GRIET Guidelines

DO'S & DON'TS OF DONATING BLOOD

Please review the official eligibility guidelines carefully before coming to the donation drive.

👍

DO'S

  • ✓
    Be in good health and have healthy and iron-rich foods prior to donation.
  • ✓
    Eat 3 hours prior to donation to maintain steady energy levels.
  • ✓
    Make sure you are 18 years old and weigh at least 45 Kgs.
  • ✓
    Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water and take adequate rest the night before.
  • ✓
    Ensure to share your medical history and current medications truthfully with the doctors.
👎

DON'TS

  • ✗
    Don't donate if you have any active health issues or chronic illnesses.
  • ✗
    Don't donate if you are under 17 years of age or underweight (< 45 kg).
  • ✗
    Don't drink alcohol or smoke within 48 hours prior to donating blood.
  • ✗
    Don't donate if you had a tattoo or piercing within the last 1 year.
  • ✗
    Don't donate if you have cold, sore throat, fever, or any other infection.
  • ✗
    Girls are advised not to donate blood immediately before, during, or right after menstrual periods.
Medical Protocol Notice: Final eligibility to donate blood will be assessed and confirmed by the authorized medical and blood-bank team at the GRIET Campus event.
Care & Comfort

Before, During & After Donation

Here is what you can expect during every phase of your donation journey.

Phase 1: Before

Preparation

  • Drink 500ml water before arriving
  • Eat a light meal 2-3 hours prior
  • Get a sound 7-8 hours sleep
  • Carry College ID / Govt Photo ID
Phase 2: During

At the Bed

  • Follow medical staff instructions
  • Relax and squeeze the sponge gently
  • Takes only 8-10 minutes for collection
  • Inform staff immediately if feeling dizzy
Phase 3: After

Post-Donation

  • Rest at observation area for 10 mins
  • Enjoy complimentary fruit juice & snacks
  • Avoid heavy lifting for rest of the day
  • Collect Donor Certificate from NSS desk
