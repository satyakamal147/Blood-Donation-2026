Frequently Asked Questions

Find immediate answers to common questions about eligibility, registration, and the event.

Any healthy individual who is at least 18 years old and weighs at least 45 kg is welcome to register. Both students and faculty/staff of GRIET as well as authorized campus visitors are eligible.
The event is hosted at the GRIET Campus, Bachupally, Hyderabad. The online registration deadline is 21 September 2026.
Simply click the REGISTER NOW button, fill in your details (name, roll number, blood group, preferred time slot), and submit. You will receive an instant unique Registration ID (e.g. GRIET-BD-0042).
Stay well hydrated by drinking 500ml of water, eat a healthy meal 2 to 3 hours prior to donation, and ensure at least 7-8 hours of sleep the night before.
Do not consume alcohol or smoke for at least 48 hours prior to donation. Avoid donating if you have active fever/cold or if you have had a tattoo or piercing within the past 12 months.
Please carry your GRIET Student / Staff Identity Card or any valid Government Photo ID (Aadhaar / Driving License / Voter ID) along with your Registration ID.
Yes! You can visit the Check Registration page and search using your Registration ID or your 10-digit mobile number at any time.
Please consult the authorized medical and blood-bank team at the event. Final eligibility to donate blood will be assessed and confirmed by licensed medical practitioners on-site.
If you are unable to attend during your allotted slot, you can reach out to the NSS Help Desk coordinators at the venue or contact the NSS team.
